# Flash privacy policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Flash is a Chrome extension that lets you ask a YouTube video a question and jump to the answer.
It has no servers of its own, no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

## What Flash sends, and where

| Data | Sent to | When |
|---|---|---|
| The transcript of the YouTube video you're watching, and your question | TypeSafe (api.typesafe.ai), using **your own** TypeSafe API key | When you ask a question, and once when a video without chapters loads |
| Your voice, in **Instant** mode | Google, through Chrome's built-in speech recognition | Only while you hold Control to talk |
| Your voice recording, in **Accurate** mode | OpenAI (api.openai.com), using **your own** OpenAI API key | Only while you hold Control to talk, and only if you added an OpenAI key |

Flash does not send your browsing history, the videos you watch, or anything else to anyone.
Those services handle the data under their own privacy policies:
[TypeSafe](https://typesafe.ai), [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[OpenAI](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy).

## What Flash stores on your computer

- **Your API keys** (TypeSafe, and OpenAI if you add one), in Chrome's extension storage on
  your device. They are sent only to their own service, to make your requests.
- **Your voice mode choice** (Instant or Accurate).
- **Search results for the current browser session**, so asking the same thing twice is instant.
  Chrome clears them when you close the browser.

Removing the extension deletes all of it.

## What Flash never does

- It never sells or shares your data.
- It never uses your data for advertising, credit checks or anything unrelated to finding
  answers in the video you're watching.
- It never records audio unless you are holding Control to talk.
